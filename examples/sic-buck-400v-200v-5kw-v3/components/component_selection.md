# V3 component decisions

Engineering prototype review material. Values below are source facts or explicitly labelled estimates; nothing is a hardware qualification. Download hashes and retrieval records are in `requirements/source_manifest.json`. Manufacturer documents are cached locally, not redistributed.

## Power switch and drive

The comparison is between Wolfspeed [C4MS025120K](https://assets.wolfspeed.com/uploads/2025/11/Wolfspeed_C4MS025120K_data_sheet.pdf) and [C3M0030090K](https://assets.wolfspeed.com/uploads/2024/01/Wolfspeed_C3M0030090K_data_sheet.pdf). Both offer a separate Kelvin-source lead. The selected 1200 V C4 provides 400 V rating margin above the provisional 800 V switching-peak target; the 900 V alternative provides only 100 V. This is not proof that the assembled converter stays below 800 V. C4 ratings include 86 A at 25°C and 62 A at 100°C case with 18 V gate drive, 25/33 mΩ typical/maximum at 25°C, 47 mΩ typical at 175°C, 125 nC typical gate charge, 30 nC gate-drain charge, and 0.41 K/W typical junction-case resistance. The tab is drain; pins are D, power S, Kelvin S, G. Package lead and thermal-fixture fit require physical review.

The new bias is nominal +17.167/-4.006 V. Independent supply and 0.1% resistor tolerances give a positive static range of 16.800–17.535 V, leaving some space below the recommended 18 V upper operating drive. The retrieved RDS(on) guarantee is at 18 V, not at this entire tolerance band: the loss estimate adds an explicit 10% assumption to the temperature-scaled maximum; this must be replaced by measurement or vendor data. The selected 10 Ω on / 3.3 Ω off resistors are conservative initial values, not a validated optimum.

[TI UCC21710-Q1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ucc21710-q1.pdf) is retained on circuit merits: ±10 A peak drive, a dedicated Miller clamp, source-referenced COM, fault/reset reporting and high CMTI. Its OC threshold is 0.63–0.77 V; maximum deglitch and OC-to-output times are included in the behavioural fault calculation. CLMPI connects directly to the gate; COM returns only to the Kelvin lead. The OC pin is internally clamped during off-state, supporting the selected external drain-voltage detection network. Timing specified under 5 V-side test conditions must not be represented as measured 3.3 V-side performance. No guaranteed C4 short-circuit withstand duration was found; DESAT functionality does not close short-circuit survival.

[TI UCC14141-Q1](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ucc14141-q1.pdf) supplies each gate island independently. The asymmetric RLIM network is 1 kΩ in parallel with 180 Ω plus a diode. Calculations include effective capacitance, imbalance, gate charge and a deliberately stressed internal-resistance assumption. The sensing supply uses a separate 82 Ω RLIM network for three AMC1311 inputs. Datasheet certification statements in the retrieved revision include planned items; no completed system certification is claimed.

## Energy storage

Four [Magnetics 0058192A2](https://www.mag-inc.com/Media/Magnetics/Datasheets/0058192A2.pdf) High Flux 60 µ cores with 28 turns AWG10 are selected over the evaluated two/three-core alternatives. The stack trades volume and mass for lower copper loss and more inductance under DC bias. Incremental inductance is about 391 µH at 25 A using the manufacturer's typical bias fit, versus 432.8 µH at zero current. The calculation sweeps AL ±8%, current and input voltage. The guaranteed 50% permeability point supplies a separate conservative fault-screening bound. This is a custom wound and chassis-supported component: winding insulation, fill, AC loss, actual bias and temperature need sample qualification.

The DC link is four TDK B32776G9156K000, 15 µF/900 V each. The output is two B32778G4107K000, 100 µF/450 V each. The [TDK family data](https://www.tdk-electronics.tdk.com/inf/20/20/db/fc_2009/MKP_B32774_778.pdf) provides the four-lead package dimensions and ripple/ESR data; equal sharing is an assumption. The calculated bank RMS current is roughly 12.7 A input and 1.8 A output. Catalogue ripple ratings at 70°C/10 kHz are not a measured 50 kHz lifetime result. Eight 0.47 µF/630 V X7T 2220 ceramics form the local HF bank. Their effective capacitance at 440 V remains a procurement/measurement blocker; no nominal-capacitance credit is used to claim commutation-loop performance.

## Sensing and protection

[LEM HO 50-S-0100](https://www.lem.com/sites/default/files/products_datasheets/ho-50__250-s_v7.pdf) replaces the prior PCB-current-neck topology. Its 16 mV/A output is conditioned with a buffered reference and gain two around 1.5 V, giving ±46.875 A ADC span. The 130–300 Ω reference output must be buffered. Sensor 3.5 µs response is typical, not a maximum. The precision ±38 A comparator window is separate from the sensor's much higher internal OCD threshold. A 4.7 kΩ pull-up **at the sensor end** of the cable and a board-side pull-down make cable disconnection inhibit PWM; this resistor is a mandatory harness BOM item. Analogue wire opens are weakly biased toward detectable faults and firmware checks the reference voltage.

Three [AMC1311B](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/amc1311.pdf) channels independently measure raw input, DC link and output. Dividers use four or two 750 kΩ resistors and 10 kΩ bottoms. The OPA4192 differential receivers run from 5 V; OPA2320 buffers/conditions current. TLV3202 comparators run at 3.3 V with resistor/Schottky protection from the 5 V analogue channels. Comparator thresholds, ADC clamps and sensor-reference errors are included in the review; power-sequence backfeeding and input-clamp currents require bench checks.

TE EV200HAANA contactors and a chassis-mounted HS100 2K2 F resistor implement input precharge. A KLKD020 input fuse is cable/fire protection pending source-energy and interruption coordination. No MOV is populated: a nominal 385 VAC MOV can clamp too high for the 630 V HF ceramics. The present integration envelope therefore requires a controlled source with terminal transients ≤500 V. OV inhibition does not clamp input surge energy. Reverse-powered output operation is excluded. Contactor auxiliary contacts are wetted above their 100 mA minimum using two parallel 200 Ω/2 W resistors. Verified Vishay [SSC54-E3/57T](https://www.vishay.com/docs/88885/ssc53l.pdf), SMC, provides external coil suppression; actual release time includes the contactor economizer.

## Procurement status

Board BOMs are generated directly from the editable circuit models. Copper net ties are excluded from procurement. Pin maps and physical pad mappings are checked, but no distributor stock or purchasing approval is implied. Precision-resistor ordering suffixes, connector mating/keys, capacitor DC-bias curves, vendor STEP models, current-sensor cable and all chassis hardware require the documented procurement/assembly review before release.

The UCC14141 DWN land is an explicit assembly variant: 0.40 mm pad width at 0.65 mm pitch, versus TI's 0.45 mm example. Its 0.25 mm adjacent-pad copper gap preserves the predeclared local rule; the 8.10 mm primary-secondary gap is unchanged. The maximum lead width is 0.39 mm, so this variant has very little lateral fillet allowance and **requires stencil, placement-offset, toe/heel wetting and solder-joint qualification**. No native clearance exception is used. The body-height envelope is corrected to the manufacturer's 3.55 mm maximum. This footprint must not be advertised as an unchanged recommended TI land pattern.
