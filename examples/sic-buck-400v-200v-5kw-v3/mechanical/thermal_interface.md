# Thermal and insulation fixture — before final placement

This is a designed assembly proposal with explicit missing qualifications. It is not a certified thermal interface or approval to fabricate.

The common sink candidate is [Fischer SK 56 200 SA, manufacturer sheet](https://www.farnell.com/datasheets/4395992.pdf): 300 mm wide, 40 mm fin depth, 10 mm base, 200 mm extrusion length. The plotted natural-convection resistance at 200 mm is approximately 0.30 K/W; the listed 0.59–0.30 range covers different lengths. Localised two-device heating, enclosure, orientation and duct losses are not included in that catalogue curve. The analytical nominal screen uses 0.35 K/W and the assembly review must also evaluate 0.45 and 0.60 K/W.

The sink stands behind the upper edge of the power PCB, with fins/extrusion vertical and the base facing the devices. It is PE bonded. Two custom copper blocks transmit heat from the vertical TO-247 tabs to a common unpunctured AlN insulating plate. This pushes the sink behind the board edge while preserving straight, short leads. It avoids a bent-lead assumption or a thin pad whose edge is too close to the live tab. Anodising is not counted as insulation.

Provisional power-board coordinates (millimetres, top copper z=0): Q1/Q2 pin-row centres (105,14)/(130,14); body bottom z=6, envelope top z=29.63. Each copper contact block has a 16×14 mm contact face, 12 mm thickness along y, and starts at z=15; y range -0.655…11.345 includes the 0.05 mm tab-side grease allowance. Blocks are separate live-drain parts, with 9 mm nominal separation between 16 mm wide faces. The selected contact patch must be checked against the manufacturer's exposed-tab drawing/STEP before manufacture; the body-envelope model alone is insufficient. Copper conductivity 390 W/mK is an engineering assumption: block resistance is approximately 0.137 K/W per device before spreading/contact losses.

The proposed custom AlN plate is 110×50×1 mm, x=65…175 and z=-2…48, behind both blocks. [CeramTec Alunit AlN HP](https://www.ceramtec-group.cn/fileadmin/user_upload/Corporate/11_Downloads/12_Substrate/CeramTec_Material_Properties_Alunit_AlN_HP_-_Substrates.pdf) lists 170 W/mK and 15 kV/mm test-sample breakdown data. Those are material-characterisation values, not a guaranteed rating for this custom plate or this assembly. At the 16×14 mm area, one-dimensional ceramic resistance is about 0.026 K/W. Three grease interfaces, flatness, clamp pressure and spreading must fit within the provisional total case-to-sink target of 0.5 K/W. Sweep 0.3–1.0 K/W in review; reject the design if the qualified result exceeds the temperature budget.

Allow nominal 0.05 mm grease films at tab/block, block/ceramic and ceramic/sink. The sink's front face is then about y=-1.755 and its bottom at z=10. The nearest unshielded top-edge copper at y=1 is about 10.37 mm from the sink's bottom edge in this ideal geometry; actual solder protrusion, tolerance and metal fasteners must be included. Ceramic edge margins from the block are at least 17 mm vertically and 32 mm laterally; no screw pierces the ceramic. A PEEK pressure bar, with metal anchors outside the live/ceramic envelope, presses the package fronts. Clamp force, polymer creep, ceramic edge chips, grease migration and retention under vibration are unresolved mechanical qualifications. Do not substitute a through-bolt through the live tab/ceramic.

The standard footprint envelopes show the devices, not the clamp/contact-block details. A later assembly representation must include separate copper blocks, ceramic, sink, board, standoffs and heavy parts. Solid-insulation hipot/impulse testing and an application-standard review remain mandatory even if geometric clearances pass.

The cooling fan candidate is Sanyo Denki [9GA0812P4G001](https://products.sanyodenki.com/en/sanace/dc/dc-fan/9GA0812P4G001/), 80×80×25 mm, 12 V, 5.76 W, 67.4 CFM maximum free-air flow and 150 Pa maximum static pressure. These two maxima do not occur simultaneously. The manufacturer catalogue is hashed in the source manifest. Provide a duct and a second airflow branch over the choke/capacitors. Fan selection is provisional until a pressure-drop operating point is measured. At 20 CFM, 107 W gives roughly 10 K ideal bulk air rise (ρ=1.1 kg/m³, cp=1005 J/kgK assumptions); that does not establish component surface temperatures. Brown PWM lead is insulated/open for the initial full-speed configuration, pending confirmation for the exact ordered fan revision; verify tach pulse scaling before enabling the converter.

Use a normally closed 90°C sink thermostat and a normally closed 110°C choke thermostat in the fail-open hardware chain. They protect against gross thermal faults, not semiconductor sub-millisecond overstress. Fan-tach supervision is firmware dependent; the thermostats are independent. The 30 A/1 s overload is a transient target and requires thermal impedance data/test; a steady-state 30 A calculation cannot qualify it.
