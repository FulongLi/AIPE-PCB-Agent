# V3 assembly design input

Before final routing, the independent power PCB starts at 250×180×2 mm and the control board target at 160×120×1.6 mm. Each has four independent M3 insulating standoffs; no stacked-board overhang supports a connector or magnetics. Boards and bare terminations remain inside a PE-bonded, tool-access enclosure. The aperture sensor and ~1 kg wound choke have their own chassis brackets. Do not support either from PCB solder joints.

The thermal fixture is defined in `thermal_interface.md`. The power board has a continuous rectangular outline; the 12 mm copper contact blocks place the ceramic/sink behind the edge, removing the earlier need for a ceramic slot through the board. This is a clean-sheet fixture proposal, not reused V2 geometry. The complete fixture still requires vendor case geometry, clamp-load/flatness tolerances and insulation qualification.

Provisional assembly reference places the power board at (0,0), the control board below it at (45,205), and the choke/sensor to the right of the power board. The keyed harness leaves the central control-only part of the power board at z≥25 mm, crosses the bottom isolation region in free air above low-profile parts, and reaches the control board within a 100 mm cable length. No high-voltage conductor travels in this harness. The final connector orientation and cable bend/engagement volume must be measured, not inferred from the 2D courtyard.

The chassis accommodates the 300×200×40 mm vertical sink, both supported boards, contactors, fuse holders, precharge resistor and insulated choke assembly. Final bounding dimensions follow the assembly model; there is no claim that V3 is smaller than V2.2. Reserve at least 8 mm from live terminals/windings to accessible or PE metal and from hazardous wiring to control wiring, including fasteners and cable motion. A rigid touch guard covers power terminals and the choke. The isolated control cable stays in its own clipped route, away from switch-node and contactor wiring.

HS100 2K2 F precharge resistor mounting uses the manufacturer's 88×47.5×24.1 mm terminal/body envelope and four 4.4 mm holes at 35×37 mm pitch. The [ARCOL/Ohmite sheet](https://media.digikey.com/pdf/Data%20Sheets/Ohmite%20PDFs/HS_Resistors.pdf) gives 100 W on a 995 cm², 3 mm standard heatsink at 25°C and only 30 W without a heatsink. Mount to a separate suitable chassis area using thermal compound; do not assume pulse-energy capability from another HS series. Validate the 1 s shorted-precharge pulse and repeated-start temperature with the actual thermal plate.

Use separately supported TE EV200HAANA main/precharge contactors. Their coil branch has an external 8 A fuse; electronics use a separate protected branch. Supply is isolated 12 V/10 A. Both coils may draw 7.6 A during the specified inrush interval, plus the fan/electronics; supply dynamic response and wire drops need qualification. The copper-block fixture, bus wiring, precharge plate, ceramic and winding are custom assemblies, not procurement-ready stock parts.

Outstanding fit inputs are explicit: exact Wolfspeed tab STEP/contact patch, Fischer fin/machining model, clamp pressure and deflection, ceramic flatness/edge quality, wound-choke envelope and insulation, sensor aperture/wire bend radii, EV200 terminal covers/torque, fuse-holder DC voltage and arc clearances, keyed-harness latch/engagement, PCB fabricator hole/stackup tolerances, and actual airflow pressure curve. Approximate boxes are useful for interference screening but do not close these items.
