# Assembly clearance and manufacturing disposition

**Design envelope reviewed; mechanical and insulation qualification remain open.** The native boards, component bodies and installation envelope are different evidence levels.

The independently supported power/control boards measure 250 × 180 × 2 mm and 160 × 120 × 1.6 mm. Both have four mounting holes. The reserved installation volume is 470 × 455 × 260 mm, including a 300 × 200 × 40 mm sink, supported choke/sensor, contactors, covered fuse holder, precharge plate and cable space. This is larger than either PCB and is not a compactness improvement claim.

`assembly_envelope.json` and `models/v3_assembly_envelopes.step` define 30 dimensional envelopes. The generic AABB screen examines 435 pairs and reports no unexplained intersections. Two 0.105 mm PEEK/body overlaps explicitly represent intended clamp contact, not a validated preload. Rounded bodies, tabs, lugs, fins, fasteners, wound insulation and wiring are not exact solids. The STEP is an editable dimensional reservation, not a fabrication drawing.

The nominal live copper-block separation is 9 mm. The unpunctured AlN plate provides at least 17 mm vertical and 32 mm lateral edge margin from a block; these are nominal edge-path dimensions, not qualified creepage. The sink front/bottom geometry gives approximately 10.37 mm from top-edge PCB copper to PE metal in the ideal model. Copper blocks transfer heat from the live device tabs; the sink is PE bonded. A metal through-bolt through the tab/ceramic is prohibited by this fixture concept. Clamp loads, thermal expansion, PEEK creep, ceramic chips, flatness, grease migration and mounting tolerances must be resolved before release.

The board bottoms are 20 mm above the baseplate. Solder protrusion, mounting screw reach, conductive debris and mechanical deflection must be included in the final clearance stack. The two board edges have a 10 mm nominal gap. Each standoff supports its own board; the control board has no unsupported overhang carrying the power board or choke.

The 160 mm finished, 40-way IDSD harness has a 128.76 mm proposed centreline and a 50.8 mm ribbon width. The centreline does not represent the swept volume. Use insulating clips to control orientation and sag over the bottom isolation region; verify connector latch engagement, pin 1, bend radius and every conductor against `system_ports.json` and the native wiring schematic. The approximately 148 mm SW-terminal-to-choke-centre distance is before lug and bend allowances and remains an EMI risk. Use supported, insulated 10 AWG power wiring rated at least 600 V and 105°C, with actual outside diameter compatible with the LEM aperture. The final wire route, covers and strain relief remain open.

The PCB project sets four 70 µm power layers and four 35 µm control layers. Stack-up thickness, copper tolerance, plated-hole thickness, high-CTI laminate availability, CAF/impulse performance, solder mask, via tenting and connector mounting require fabricator review. The UCC14141 0.40 mm lands are a documented assembly variant of TI's 0.45 mm pattern, with a 0.39 mm maximum lead. A clean spacing check does not establish solder-joint capability; alignment/stencil/fillet inspection is a release hold. Thermal-pad vias on the control board need an agreed stencil and via treatment.

Exact missing models: Wolfspeed tab/contact geometry and lead-to-body tolerances; Fischer fins/machining; the clamp/fasteners and AlN edges; wound choke and its insulation; LEM aperture/terminals and wire bends; contactor/fuse-holder terminal covers; keyed cable engagement. The power-board L2/L3/L4 common-mode choke model path is unavailable in this installed KiCad library. U3 on the control board uses an explicitly approximate 4.5 × 9.8 × 1.2 mm PWP body envelope; its lead/exposed-pad geometry is defined in the footprint, not the envelope model.

F2 is selected as Littelfuse KLKD008.T, 8 A /600 VDC, in a separate L60030M1C holder with SPL001 cover; inrush, hot derating and source/fuse coordination remain open. The [2025-05-29 manufacturer sheet](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/klkd-fuse-datasheet?assetguid=7b52eebb-c7cc-43df-a8da-9682a338e0f6) gives 19.4 mΩ nominal cold resistance and 130 A²s average melting I²t. The two 3.8 A coil pickups plus 0.48 A rated fan would give about 8.49 A²s over 130 ms if constant; actual fan starting current, repetitive pulse life and a current-limited source are not validated by that simple comparison. Sensata 67L090P/67L110P are open-on-rise candidates; their actual 3.3 V / 3.3 mA contact loading is below the cited 5 V / 10 mA life-test condition and requires dry-contact reliability qualification. Fan PWM-open behavior, tach scaling and coil pickup under the simultaneous 7.6 A inrush remain supplier/hardware checks.

No fabrication, procurement, assembly approval or energization is authorized by these geometric screens.
