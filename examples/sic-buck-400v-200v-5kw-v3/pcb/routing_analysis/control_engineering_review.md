# Control PCB routed review

The native four-layer 160 × 120 mm board has 266 footprints. Native ERC, DRC and schematic parity are separate from the following engineering assessment. The copper evidence is recorded in `control_return_projection.json`; input hashes bind it to the actual board.

The 30 MHz oscillator-to-series-resistor branch is 3.67 mm with no vias. The resistor-to-DSP clock branch is 6.12 mm with two vias; the nearest ground connection to each transition is 1.15 and 1.01 mm away. The reference-plane projection has 1.50 mm uncovered, including required signal-via antipads. This is a short source-terminated route with a measurable return context, not a controlled-impedance or eye-diagram result. Oscillator package model compatibility was corrected without changing pads or routing.

The DSP EPWM_A/B branches total 46.08 / 37.91 mm with 3 / 5 vias. Buffer-to-harness PWM_H/L branches are 86.88 / 87.06 mm with 0 / 1 vias. The nominally similar output lengths do not prove matched delay. The longest nearest-ground distance among EPWM_B transitions is 4.85 mm. The connector and 160 mm ribbon cable must be tested together with termination, ground allocation, receiver thresholds and the actual edge rates. The 50 kHz repetition rate is not the signal-bandwidth limit.

CURRENT_OK totals 87.63 mm and five vias; its longest transition-to-ground distance is 2.65 mm. ARMED totals 37.90 mm and two vias. These asynchronous protection paths terminate in independent trip-zone inputs. Their physical length is small compared with the unverified microsecond sensor/shutdown budget, but no propagation-delay sign-off is inferred without a timing measurement. EMI-induced glitches, missed trips, brownouts, reset behavior and power-sequencing faults require injection tests.

ADC_BUS and ADC_V12 have continuous geometric In1 ground projection along 11.30 / 22.81 mm of trace. Other ADC branches span approximately 23–36 mm and use two or three vias. Reported projection gaps include the signal vias' own antipads; they are not all split-plane crossings. The 220 Ω / 1 nF filters, source impedance, DSP acquisition window, reference stability and one-cycle sample delay must be evaluated together. Ground-filled geometry is not proof of low-noise conversion under SiC common-mode transients. Reference branches ADCREFP/M are 4.82 / 6.76 mm; their via-to-ground distances are at most 1.81 / 1.00 mm. ADCRESEXT is 14.54 mm with two vias and remains a targeted impedance/noise measurement item.

Per-supply-pin DSP bypasses are placed behind the pin field and connect through local escapes. Core-first sequencing and the TPS767D301 output-capacitor ESR network are explicit circuit choices. C2's final input-bypass location is on the back at (20.25,16.75), with separate supply and return vias; the earlier front-side intent is superseded. LDO transient response, supply impedance, MCU reset margins, amplifier startup/back-power behavior and ADC clipping recovery remain bench qualifications.

In1 is the reference-ground plane; In2 also carries supply distribution and signal routing. A back-side signal is therefore not automatically a stripline with one uninterrupted nearby reference. JTAG, serial communications and the unisolated accessible-control connectors must stay within the enclosure's control-domain assumptions. The isolated auxiliary source and power-board isolation are system requirements; the control PCB alone does not make a hazardous installation touch safe.

The ground polygon is geometrically connected, but this review is not an electromagnetic field extraction. Required hardware evidence includes rail/reference noise, oscillator startup and waveform, ADC settling/calibration, PWM overshoot, trip timing, connector intermittency and common-mode immunity.
