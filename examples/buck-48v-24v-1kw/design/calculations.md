## Sensing and thresholds

Vin: 121 kohm / 4.99 kohm divider => 2.139 V at 54 V. Vout: 49.9 kohm / 4.99 kohm => 2.182 V at 24 V. Buffer voltage channels before the ADC sampling capacitors.
2 mohm four-terminal shunt and INA240A1 gain 20 => 1.667 V at full load. Hardware OCP nominal 60.0 A; OVP 27.5 V; LM61 overtemperature 90.0 C. Comparator and reference tolerances, amplifier offset, shunt heating and dynamic delay must be included in test limits.
At 54 V into a short with 6.4 uH, di/dt is 8.44 A/us. The sensing/comparator chain is not certified short-circuit protection: even 3 us adds 25.3 A. The input fuse limits sustained faults, and a current-limited source is an explicit boundary condition. A fuse does not protect the MOSFET from all fast faults.
