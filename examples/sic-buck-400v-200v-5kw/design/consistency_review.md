## 4. Baseline issues reported, not edited (orchestrator items)

1. Mechanical height: `architecture.mechanical` sums "15 + 2 + 55 = 72 mm" while `architecture.power_board.heatsink` and `thermal` give the sink as 50 mm high; the documents use 67 mm and flag the 5 mm difference.
2. Corridor part height: placement rule 4 and the heatsink entry allow parts <= 10 mm tall in the intake / exhaust corridors, placement rule 7 (added in schema 3) says <= 15 mm. The documents keep 10 mm (stricter, stated twice).
3. Placement rule 4 (">= 10 mm from the sink") and rule 7 (">= 20 mm") coexist; the documents apply the binding 20 mm (CR-23). Rule 4 should be deleted or reworded.
4. NTC reference: `sensing.heatsink_temperature.route` says "2.2 k pull-up to 3.00 V (REF3125 buffered)"; a 2.5 V REF3125 cannot supply 3.00 V without a gain stage. component_selection.md and component_records.json carry a REF3130 (3.0 V) as an added assumption; architecture.md now cross-references it. The baseline should name the 3.00 V source.
5. Fault combiner: `protection.fault_combiner` says "2 x SN74LV21A"; 13 latched inputs plus the 4-input run-permit AND need 6 four-input gates = 3 packages (component_selection.md) or a fifth gate from another part (architecture.md). Both documents flag it.
6. NTC thresholds: with the schema 3 pad value the baseline's own mapping (+50-66 K) puts the 100 C firmware trip at T_J,HS 149-156 C and the 110 C hardware trip at 176-178 C at the max-R overload corner, i.e. above the 150 C design limit and the 175 C absolute maximum respectively. The thresholds remain "review proposals" in the baseline; the thermal and firmware documents now state the consequence and offer two proposals (overload inhibit at 90 C; a lower 84 / 94 / 104 C ladder or the T_J estimator).
7. Single-width LAM 5 K 100 12 cost-down option: the baseline condition "only if measured R_pad <= 1 K/W" is now the only admissible case; with the 1.4 K/W design value it fails the 150 C limit (152.6 C). Consistent with the baseline text, but worth stating in `thermal.heatsink`.
8. LS tab displacement current: `power_stage.mosfet.tab_potentials` keeps "2.3 A" (TSP 3500 capacitance); with the TSP A2000 (Dk 7.0, 56-82 pF) the thermal document computes 2.8-4.1 A at 50 V/ns (6-9 A at the quoted 110-120 V/ns).
9. Fan-fail time: `thermal.results.fan_fail` "sink 68 -> 100 C in about 50-100 s" is kept as a conservative requirement in all documents; the lumped estimates for the D K unit are 3-11 min (thermal_analysis.md, calculations.md). The conclusion (OTP mandatory) is unaffected.
10. Discharge time: `capacitors.bleeders.discharge_to_60V_s` "about 30" is the bleeder-only estimate at nominal C; the merged networks with the indicator chains give about 20 s, bleeders alone about 36 s (calculations.md cross-check "differs", both < 60 s).
11. The task text handed to this review still quoted the schema 2 pad value ("TSP A2000 1.1 K/W"); the baseline file (schema 3) was followed.
