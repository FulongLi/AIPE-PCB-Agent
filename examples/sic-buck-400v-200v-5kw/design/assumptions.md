### 4.2 Impulse withstand basis and its conflict

IEC 60664-1 Table F.1 / 61800-5-1 Table 7 (corroborated by secondary sources, SS): 300 V system -> OVC II 2.5 kV, OVC III 4 kV; 600 V system -> OVC II 4 kV, OVC III 6 kV. Design basis 4 kV for functional and basic insulation, 6 kV for reinforced (next step). The conflict is recorded, not resolved: a bus rectified from 230/400 V mains is a 300 V system, where OVC II allows 2.5 kV (1.5 mm basic / 3.0 mm reinforced clearance); a transformer-isolated laboratory source at 400 V counts itself as the system voltage and falls in the 600 V row (4 kV). The source is unspecified, so the 600 V row is adopted (A-27, H). The research record's claim that 1.6 x TOV = 4080 V "would interpolate to 6.4 mm" is not reproducible from any quoted row (verifiers obtain 3.1-5.8 mm depending on the column); the governing reinforced clearance stays 5.5 mm and the 9.0 mm rule covers every reading. Edition statement corrected: the numeric tables are those of IEC 61800-5-1:2007; the current edition is IEC 61800-5-1:2022 (Ed. 3) + COR1:2023 (+ COR2:2025), which renumbers the discharge clause to 4.4.9 - there is no "2023 edition". The research tagged Tables 3 / 9 / 10 and clauses 4.3.11 / 6.5.2 as read from the standard; the consulted copy is not on record and neither verifier could access the text, so those values are carried as secondary/unverified until a licensed copy is reviewed. Only Table F.1, IPC-2221B Table 6-1 and the IPC-2221 current formula were corroborated by secondary sources; the engineering verifier's recollection agrees with the F.2 / F.4 values below.

### 4.5 Slots under barrier parts

Every barrier part whose primary-to-secondary land gap g is below 9.0 mm (3 x UCC14141 DWN, 2 x UCC21710 DW, 2 x AMC1311 DWV, TMCS1133 DVG; g = 7.6-8.0 mm) gets a non-plated through-slot, 1.2 mm nominal and >= 1.0 mm after fabrication tolerance (the PD2 X-value; a narrower gap is bridged when measuring creepage). The research record's model "path = gap + 2 x board thickness" is wrong for a through-slot: the walls end in air, so the only surface path runs around the slot ends, path = 2 sqrt((g/2)^2 + e^2) with e the extension beyond the outermost pad edges. With e = 1 mm the path is 7.9-8.3 mm, below the rule; e >= 2.5 mm gives 9.1-9.4 mm; the adopted e >= 3 mm gives 9.7-10.0 mm [AC]. Where e = 3 mm does not fit, the local fallback is the standard-minimum PWB creepage 5.5 mm (still >= the 5.5 mm clearance), recorded per instance.
