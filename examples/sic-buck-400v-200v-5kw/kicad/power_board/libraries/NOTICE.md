# Library provenance

Project footprint library of the AIPE V2 SiC buck POWER board, generated by automation.kicad_tools.generate_sic_schematics from sic_buck_power_model.CUSTOM_FOOTPRINTS and the official KiCad 10 library. Official footprints keep their licence (LICENSE.md, CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt). Custom AIPE footprints (film capacitors, toroid, heatsink envelope, UCC14141 / AMC1311 / TMCS1133 with isolation slots, PTC envelope) are dimensioned from the sources named in the model docstring and need independent review against the manufacturer drawings; the heatsink hole pattern and the PTC are placeholders.
