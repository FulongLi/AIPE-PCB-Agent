# Exact next engineering ECO requirements

These are unresolved design work, not changes claimed by V2.2.

1. **VEEA feedback return (U2/U4/U5).** Add separate secondary analogue-ground nets. Connect pin 35, each FBVDD/FBVEE bottom resistor and its 330 pF capacitor to a local star. Exclude the power VEE plane on all layers from that star route; use one documented connection at the module's intended VEE return, following TI SLUSF10B §9.5. Re-place the feedback networks as needed, then verify graph connectivity, actual filled-copper topology, return-current separation, startup and noise injection. Merely adding a zero-ohm component while leaving a shared plane fails this ECO.

2. **Commutation loop and low-side driver shelf.** Review C1–C10, Q1/Q2 and the PGND return together. Baseline Q1/Q2 origins are (87,126)/(115,126) mm and C1 (79,125.9) mm. The intermediate region includes TP1/KS_H at (102,126), SW vias near (109,125), and D5's HV pad field. Retain the 3 mm outer HV-functional rule and the heatsink projection/keepout. Obtain a MOSFET/heatsink lead-bend assembly drawing before relocating the switching devices. Compare the *complete* VIN–Q1–Q2–PGND–capacitor loop using 3D/parasitic extraction or double-pulse measurements; reject alternatives whose only improvement is a nearer endpoint. Re-place R37/R38/R39 with a paired Kelvin return only after confirming this assembly.

3. **Power copper and sensor neck.** Analyse the 11.78 mm SW In2 neck proxy, 4.8 mm VOUT_L outer-layer link and TMCS1133 primary pads against 25 A continuous / overload current and local cooling. Obtain sensor manufacturer land-pattern/current guidance. A parallel-layer/via option must provide entry and exit arrays, controlled current sharing and unchanged isolation clearance. Validate with field/thermal modelling and infrared/contact temperature measurements. The erosion proxy cannot certify copper ampacity.

4. **Assembly and isolation.** Draw the control board's transformed 120 × 80 mm outline and all four standoffs on the power-board/chassis assembly; the current transform extends to y=234 mm versus the power-board y=180 mm edge. Add support or choose an approved new transform; verify connector engagement and inaccessible HV interfaces. Specify drain-tab insulators, creepage around screws, PE bonding and the actual heatsink projection. Resolve 9 mm design-target versus 7.24/8.09/7.74 mm barrier pin gaps with applicable-standard insulation coordination; do not equate slots with increased air clearance.

5. **Qualification before any energisation.** Select RT1 and qualify precharge/relay/fuse/MOV/discharge coordination. Validate supplies and asynchronous fault logic first with safe bench substitutes. Verify PWM default-off, brownout, loss of interconnect, dead time, OC/DESAT/OVP/OTP, fan failure and load dump with an independent engineer-approved procedure. Confirm inductor saturation, capacitor RMS current/lifetime and control-loop margins. Operating the existing PCB at rated voltage/power is not authorised by these CAD reports.
