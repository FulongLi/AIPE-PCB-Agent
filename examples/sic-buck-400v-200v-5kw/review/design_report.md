## 3. Architecture chosen and alternatives

Option B was chosen: a power board carrying the devices, drivers, three bias modules, DC link, inductor, output bank, all eight barrier parts and the fast protection, plus a SELV control board stacked on the power board's SELV strip through a 2 x 25 mezzanine ([architecture.md](../design/architecture.md) section 1). The weighted scores are A 2.75 / B 4.00 / C 3.10, and B leads in all five sensitivity sets by at least 0.37 ([architecture_comparison.md](architecture_comparison.md) section 5).

| Option | Boards | Why not chosen / chosen |
|---|---|---|
| A, integrated | one 240 x 204 mm board, 70 um on every layer | repeats V1-09: a 0.5 mm pitch LQFP-176 on 2 oz outer copper has a 0.22 mm gap with 10 % margin and no solder-mask dam (architecture_comparison.md section 4) |
| B, two boards (chosen) | power 240 x 180 mm, 4 x 70 um, 2.0 mm; control 120 x 80 mm, 4 x 35 um, 1.6 mm | one barrier line on one board (KR-ISO-001), fine-pitch part on 35 um copper, bench PWM drive of the power stage at the mezzanine |
| C, plug-in driver cards | as B plus two 50 x 30 mm driver cards | adds a connector inside each gate loop and a barrier instance per card; no programme use for the modularity (section 8) |

Residual risks of B recorded for review: the single-ended mezzanine link in a 400 V / 50 kHz environment, the stacked-board through-air clearance at PD2, and an HV zone utilisation of 99-119 % of the 33,600 mm2 HV area with the baseline heatsink reservation (CR-22; architecture_comparison.md sections 3 and 9). The placed board met the 240 x 180 mm target (section 7).

## 10. Simulation summary

The behavioural ngspice campaign (KiCad-bundled ngspice-46) ran 20 transient runs - 11 open-loop corners and 9 closed-loop runs (eight cascaded-controller transients and one module-PI reference) - plus 4 parasitic cases, cross-checked against an exact switched-model solution over the ten open-loop CCM runs: within 0.15 % on every current, voltage and loss metric except C_in RMS (2.44 %; the exact model has no input filter) and the output ripple vout_pkpk (5.7 %, about 10 mV; the exact model ignores the input-bus ripple); the light-load run is excluded from the cross-check ([simulation_report.md](../simulation/simulation_report.md) sections 1, 4, 5.2; `cross_check_max_abs_deviation_pct_open_loop_ccm_runs` in [campaign_summary.json](../simulation/results/campaign_summary.json)). It shows: inductor ripple 6.51 / 7.35 / 8.03 App at 360 / 400 / 440 V (baseline 6.5 / 7.3 / 8.0), worst 9.30 App and 32.15 A peak at 440 V / 27.5 A / 235 uH; output ripple 0.16-0.22 Vpp; input ripple 3.56-3.97 Vpp; conduction 29.7-29.9 W and dead-time 2.89 W at 400 V / 25 A; 12.5 A steps of -7.74 to +7.80 V recovering in 0.75-0.77 ms; 25 A dumps peaking at 216.2 / 215.8 V below the 230 V OVP; and that a single voltage-loop PI fails the step (-16.2 V, 5.0 ms, 38.5 A above the OC trip), so the inner current loop is required.
