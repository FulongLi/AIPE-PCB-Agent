generated CAD establishes electrical safety, standards compliance, EMC or manufacturability; no fabrication, procurement
or energisation is authorised.**

Scope: the three-board DAB-V1 design generated by `automation/dab_v1` from `examples/dab-10kw/requirements/` - a primary
power board (800 V bus), a secondary power board (400 V bus) and a control board carrying a TMS320F280039C controlCARD.

## 3. UVLO, OC/SC (DESAT), fault reporting

- **UVLO**: UCC21710 has VCC (3.3 V logic side) and VDD/VEE UVLO; RDY_x (open-drain, pulled up on the power board) reports
  a ready driver. The UCC14141 bias modules report PG (open-drain, LOW = good) on one wired-AND line PG_BIAS_N per board.
  Both reach the control board and enter the hardware fault tree.
- **DESAT / short circuit**: 2.2 k pull-up from VDD_x to the DESAT node, 1200 V ultrafast diode (STTH112A, DO-41 so the
  lead spacing carries the bus voltage on the board) to the drain, 9.09 k / 1 k divider to the OC input (0.7 V threshold),
  680 pF blanking, BAT46 negative clamp. The diode stands in the device row so that the drain-net copper is a pour
  tongue instead of a hazardous track crossing the island. **Assumption**: the V3 network values (about 6.4 V trip)
  carry over; TI set 6.4 V / 1 us with its calculator for the same driver and devices. **Review R-07**: recompute the
  blanking for 1 us, confirm short-circuit withstand of the C3M devices against detection + soft turn-off time (KR-PR-001).
- **Fault output**: FLT_x_N (open-drain, pulled up to 3V3 on the power board, 100 k pull-down on the control board so an
  unplugged harness reads as a fault) into the fault tree. The UCC21710 soft turn-off handles the local shutdown; the
  control board latch then removes every PWM.

## 6. Isolation strategy and spacing

- Every power board has exactly one reinforced barrier: a copper-free 8.0 mm band (y = 70-78 mm) across the whole board,
  crossed only by the isolation packages (UCC21710 DW, UCC14141 DWN, AMC1311/AMC1336 DWV, TMCS1133 DVG) whose AIPE
  footprints have 8.0-9.1 mm inner land gaps. The KiCad rule `Reinforced HV-to-SELV separation` (8.0 mm, all copper
  layers) is derived from the insulation table in `design_constraints.yaml`: 2 x creepage(800 V, PD2, MG I) = 8.0 mm and
  clearance(6 kV) = 5.5 mm, so 8.0 mm governs; the same 8.0 mm is kept on the 400 V secondary board as margin.
- Functional spacing between hazardous domains is computed per net-class pair from the explicit potential model
  (`a*BUS + b*SW_A + c*SW_B` plus a local envelope) and the reference table with linear interpolation (e.g. 800 V -> 4.0 mm,
  825 V -> 4.15 mm, 450 V -> 2.25 mm). Same-domain nets keep the board minimum.
- Package-limited lead fields (TO-247-4 drain/source 3.175 mm, 2220 capacitors 3.3 mm on the 800 V primary) are explicit,
  pair-specific relaxations in the `.kicad_dru` (see `design/spacing_primary-power.json` -> `relaxations`), justified by the
  manufacturer rating of the component itself. There are none on the secondary board.
- **All of these are IEC 60664-1 table values used as reference inputs and labelled to-be-reviewed (R-01/R-03): the
  applicable standard, pollution degree, CTI certificate, impulse category, solid-insulation evaluation of inner layers and
  the heat-sink insulation system are open.**

### 6.2 Gate-drive interface, protection connections and the DESAT relocation

- Gate loops: `G_x`, `KS_x`, `OUTH_x`, `OUTL_x` are routed as critical nets on F.Cu/B.Cu inside the island regions;
  the driver's CLMPI pin (pin 7) exit was the one critical connection that stayed open in the first re-run (sealed by
  `OUTL_x`); the fan-out band and exit lanes of the second re-run resolved it on the primary board except where noted
  in the results table.
- DESAT: the diode cathode moved from y = 24 mm to 32.8 mm (anode from 34.2 to 43.0 mm, the island top). The DESAT
  trace from the anode to the divider is shorter than before; the drain-side pour tongue is 9 mm longer and crosses the
  ceramic-capacitor row in a free column (DRC-clean against the 4.0 mm BUS_P-PGND / SW-PGND rules because the filler
  clips it). The blanking network values (R-07) are unchanged.
- Protection connections (`PG_BIAS_N` wired-AND, `BIAS_ENF`, `FLT_x_N`, `RDY_x`, `OC_TRIP_N`): the harness-side pull-ups
  and filters are placed and routed on the SELV strip; `BIAS_ENF` and `PG_BIAS_N` are among the connections that are
  still open at the module pins in the current boards (results table) - the protection chain is therefore not complete
  on the CAD as committed, and nothing here may be read as a working protection path.

## 7. Grounding

- GND_CTRL is the single SELV reference: a continuous In1.Cu plane on the control board and on the SELV strip of each
  power board; it is never connected to PGND of either bridge (the AMC1311/AMC1336 GND1 and the sensing bias return are
  PGND, a hazardous rail named as a ground only so that KiCad's ground-pin ERC accepts the amplifier pins).
- Each switch island is referenced to its Kelvin source; the bias module's quiet VEEA pin is joined to VEE only at one
  net-tie star (NT) at pin 36 (repository V3 lesson).
- Heat sinks are PE-bonded through the chassis; no PCB copper is a protective conductor; mounting holes take insulated
  standoffs (M3), one of them inside the barrier band.
- Review R-12: common-mode current paths (Y capacitance, harness routing, heat-sink coupling) are not modelled.

## 8. Fault latching and interlocks (hardware)

```
FLT_P_x_N (4) --AND4--> FLT_P_OK --\
FLT_S_x_N (4) --AND4--> FLT_S_OK ---\
RDY_P_x  (4)  --AND4--> RDY_P_OK ----+-- AND4 --> DRIVERS_OK --\
RDY_S_x  (4)  --AND4--> RDY_S_OK ---/                           \
PG_P_N, PG_S_N --INV--> PG_x_OK ---\                             +-- AND4 --> ALL_GOOD --> CLR_N of SN74LVC1G74
OC_TRIP_P_N, OC_TRIP_S_N -----------+-- AND4 --> PROT_OK -------/
OV_P_OK, OV_S_OK, AUX_FLT_N, NRST --- AND4 --> SUPPLY_OK ------/
ARM (card GPIO pulse) --> CLK; D = 1; Q = ARMED --> TZ input of the card; PWM_ENABLE = ARMED AND RUN_EN
PWM_ENABLE gates all eight ePWM outputs (SN74LVC2G08) before they leave the board
```

- The asynchronous clear has priority: any fault drops ALL_GOOD, clears the latch, removes PWM_ENABLE (all eight PWM
  lines go low within gate delays) and asserts the card's trip zone; re-arming requires a new ARM edge after every cause
  has cleared. The supervisor reset (NRST) also clears the latch; the unplugged-harness state reads as a fault (pull-ups /
  pull-downs chosen accordingly). Firmware cannot override the latch; it can only arm it.
- Open items: R-05 (dead time and the TZ reaction remain controller functions), R-15 (propagation delays measured end
  to end: sensor -> comparator -> latch -> gate -> driver), the eFuse FLT polarity on the control board (R-16).

## 11. Connectors and pin-outs

- Power-board harness (both boards, identical map): 40-way keyed IDC header, straight-through cable, signal/return pairs
  for the PWM lines, 2 x 12V_E, 2 x 3V3, 10 x GND_CTRL, four FLT_N, four RDY, PG_BIAS_N, OC_TRIP_N, TEMP_HS, VSNS_P/N,

- The harness carries SELV only; no gate current and no hazardous potential enters the cable.
