## What V2.1 is and is not

V2.1 is a presentation and routing-geometry revision of a CAD-complete prototype. The two
schematics are drawn with wires, junctions, power symbols and hierarchical labels in functional
groups, and both boards are routed with a final geometry pass that removes stair-steps and most
90-degree corners while keeping every V2 route decision (no net longer, no via added, no track
narrower). The netlist is identical to V2 by construction and by gate, native ERC and DRC are at
zero findings with schematic parity and all severities, and the regeneration is reproducible in
copper and zones. It is not a new design and not a validation: zero native findings prove rule
compliance in KiCad against the unchanged V2 rule set (including the 39 documented per-footprint
pin-field exceptions of V2), not electrical, thermal, EMI or insulation adequacy. The V2 open
engineering issues (EX `verification/known_issues.md`, 49 rows V2-01..V2-49; six power-board and two
control-board metric rows above their review thresholds; ten review findings NOT APPLIED) remain
open with unchanged status, because V2.1 moved no component and changed no copper width, pour or rule;
15 of the 49 rows carry an added "V2.1: value (file)" annotation where the routing changed a quoted
geometric number (register header, lines 10-11; compared with `releases/v2/verification/known_issues.md`); the
register now also carries 18 V2.1 rows (V2.1-01..V2.1-18) about the drawing and routing generation
itself, and states that none of them changes the status of a V2 row. The
8-direction search exists but is opt-in; 1121 (power) and 1233 (control) 90-degree bends remain
and are listed per corner in `routing_quality_<board>.json`. Nothing was fabricated, purchased,
energised or measured, no firmware exists, and V1 and the frozen V2 release are untouched.
