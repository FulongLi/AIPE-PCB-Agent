Rule corrections.

26. SKILL-002 / SKILL-006 (closes proposal 19): record the V2 partition pattern with its measured values. One barrier line across the full 240 mm board through all eight isolators, a 10 mm ISO_BARRIER rule area copper-free on all four layers, device row on the HV side, 30 mm SELV strip at the edge, stacked control board without any HV copper. Measured on the delivered power board (`layout_metrics_power.md`, isolation and barrier tables): minimum HV-to-SELV copper distance 9.486 / 11.100 / 11.100 / 12.025 mm on F.Cu / In1 / In2 / B.Cu against the 9.0 mm rule; HV-to-PE 5.0 mm on every layer; HS-to-LS island 6.271 / 6.397 / 4.800 / 6.397 mm; eight 1.2 mm NPTH slots with land gaps 7.25 mm (UCC21710 DW), 8.10 mm (UCC14141 DWN), 9.10 mm (AMC1311 DWV) and 7.75 mm (TMCS1133 DVG), slot extensions 3.0-3.555 mm and around-the-end surface paths 10.155 / 10.080 / 11.119 / 9.807 mm. Record the costs too, so the pattern is not copied blindly: the drivers ended on F.Cu on the barrier line 14.35 mm from the gate pads instead of on B.Cu under the devices (LR-31), 4282 mm of SELV track had to cross the power board on the reviewed board (`layout_review_findings.json` `measured_summary.power.complexity`, a block measured at 4752 tracks / 574 vias; not re-measured on the delivered board, which has 4755 / 606), and the 80 mm deep control board overhangs the 30 mm strip by 54 mm with two of four standoffs unsupported (LR-27).
27. KR-PCB-006 / PE-PCB-006: the via-count metric cannot judge high-current copper. On the delivered board the rows VIN_RAW (F.Cu 422.8 mm2 + B.Cu 422.8 mm2, 0 vias, joined through the REDCUBE and PTC through-hole pads) and VOUT_L (F.Cu 422.2 mm2, 0 vias) read "exceeds" against ">= 24 vias per net", while the real defect of the reviewed board - the input PGND return squeezed to a 4.56 mm inner-copper neck between the J6 pin field and the barrier (LR-23, blocker) - was invisible to any via count. The correction pass introduced an erosion-connectivity neck-width measurement between named terminal pairs per layer (`optimisation_before_after.md` pass 2): PGND J2 -> Q2.2 on In1 4.56 -> 24.22 mm, SW via group -> L1.1 on In2 5.22 -> 18.48 mm, and it also exposed a loss, VIN C6.1 -> Q1.1 on F.Cu 5.05 -> 2.35 mm after the rule-aware refill. Proposal: give KR-PCB-006 a verification procedure "neck width between every terminal pair of a high-current net, per layer, compared with the IPC-2221 width for the design temperature rise" (the V2 baseline values are 12 mm outer / 22 mm inner per 25 A at 70 um, `design/pcb_stackup.md` section 6) and report THT-joined layer transitions separately from via arrays.
28. KR-ISO-002: add the pin-field exception pattern to the verification procedure. The 3.0 mm outer functional rule cannot be met inside a TO-247-4 land pattern (2.54 mm pitch), between the pads of 2512 / 1812 snubber, bleeder and damping parts, or at an 0805 LED (1.875 mm pitch). V2 handled this with per-footprint rules (exceptions of 0.9-2.7 mm outer copper at the film / snubber / bleeder / damping pad pairs and 4.69 mm at R14 / R16 to PE, LR-31), a PGND_LV class for the LED anode nodes of the indicator strings, and Default-class exceptions for no-connect pads because KiCad puts `unconnected-*` nets into the Default class (commit `ea8e874`, `generate_sic_schematics.py`). The checker still reports VIN-PGND and VOUT-PGND at 1.95 mm on F.Cu inside the R6 / R10 pin fields as "exceeds" (`layout_metrics_power.md`). Proposal: state in KR-ISO-002 that every class-pair rule needs a documented per-footprint exception list where the package pitch is below the rule, that the exceptions are review items rather than violations, that the layout checker must read the same list, and that `unconnected-*` nets need an explicit class or exception.
29. KR-TH-002 needs a numeric clause and a mechanical-item clause (extends proposal 13). V2 took the 5.0 mm HV-to-PE distance from the standards record, not from KR-TH-002, and nobody applied it to the heatsink's four 3.2 mm NPTH mounting holes: NPTH pads carry no net, so neither the `.kicad_dru` HV-to-PE rule nor the checker saw them, and the inner PGND and VIN pours came within 0.25 mm of the hole walls with PE-bonded hardware through them (LR-21, blocker). The fix was four all-layer rule areas `HS1_HOLE_1..4` (hole wall to HV copper on In1 / In2 0.25 -> 5.1 mm, pass 2 table). Proposal: KR-TH-002 (or a new KR-TH-004) should say "treat every PE-bonded mechanical item - sink outline, clips, standoffs, PE stud and mounting holes including NPTH - as a PE conductor subject to the HV-to-PE distance on all layers, implemented as rule areas because NPTH has no net", and require the design to derive and record the number even though the rule itself carries none.
30. New verification rule for SKILL-002 step 9 ("Run DRC and inspect every copper layer") and KR-ISO-002: stored fills and refilled fills must both pass. The reviewed power board had a 0 / 0 / 0 `kicad-cli` DRC with `--refill-zones` while the zone fills stored in the file - the copper plotted to gerbers, PDFs, renders and STEP and measured by the checker - violated the project rules in 499 places at 0.30 mm (LR-20; `pcb_rule_review.md` section 1), because the generator's in-process fill used the zone clearance only. The refill changed the copper materially (F.Cu VIN 5690.4 -> 4530.4 mm2, B.Cu PGND 10558.5 -> 9042.0 mm2, pass 2 table). V2 now refills through a fresh KiCad Python process with the project rules loaded, saves that copper, and records a no-refill DRC in `engineering_checks/stored_zone_fill_check_<board>.json` (0 / 0 for both boards). Proposal: make "DRC of the stored fills without refill is zero before any export" a project rule (PE-PCB-009) and a verifier check, and note in SKILL-002 that an in-memory DRC zero says nothing about the exported copper.
31. PE-PCB-002 / KR-CAP-003: a one-sided HF tier defeats the rule even when one row passes. The baseline asked for the HV ceramics "on both sides of the device row"; the floorplan placed all ten CGA9P 2220 capacitors west of Q1 in two rows, so Q1 drain to the nearest same-net HF pad is 8.37 mm but Q2 source to the nearest PGND HF pad is 41.2 mm, the loop closes through the In1 plane 41-79 mm back under the tier, and the F.Cu VIN pour along the tier narrows to 2.35 mm after the refill (LR-22; `layout_metrics_power.md` commutation table). The correction could not be applied because the sink keep-out, the SW In2 neck and the LS island's routing regions left no VIN path east of Q2 (`optimisation_finding_status_pass2.json`). Proposal: express PE-PCB-002 as two measurable rows - HS drain pad and LS source pad each within 15 mm of a same-net HF capacitor pad, both sides populated - and make the floorplan stage reserve the second column before routing regions are cut; record the review's board-loop estimate of 6-8 nH against the 36 nH total budget from the simulation report as the quantity the rule protects.
32. KR-PCB-001 / KR-PCB-002 and SKILL-002 procedure step 3: the HS-side recipe should be the stated method. The HS gate loop came out at 26.4 mm of track with 0 vias, 70 % of its copper over the Kelvin pour; the LS gate loop at 33.1 mm with 7 vias (84 mm of gate-network copper against 40 mm, 57 % over the Kelvin pour, R41 / TP2 over the SW In2 pour; LR-24). Restricting the LS nets to B.Cu failed in build r20c because the driver's stub-end ports were F.Cu stubs and a single-layer region allows no via (9 open connections, manifest `20261011T053341Z-drc-ba043c49`). Proposal: add to SKILL-002 "pre-route the gate-network rows directly above the driver's stub vias on the Kelvin-pour layer before signal routing; keep R_G, clamp links and probe points off the switch-node pour" and adopt the measurable "over_kelvin_pour fraction >= 0.7" that the review used.

Tooling and process.

33. Shared-router version drift: the control board was delivered with `grid_router.py` at sha256 `b78a49cc48f2c82656a382e94078d9cb442a54ad4d3e68f7237161798c48c7b3` (`shared_router_sha256` in `verification/routing_progress_control.json`); the module changed to `c666ba73...` during the power-board convergence. A scratch regeneration with the current module (EX `verification/engineering_checks/control_regeneration_check.json`) reproduced every track, via, footprint and zone of the delivered board (0 items present in only one board; file hashes differ through uuids and timestamps only), so the drift had no geometric effect in this case - but that was established by a separate check, not by the record. Proposal: every generator records the hash of each automation module it imports in its progress file, and `verify_sic_buck` reports a mismatch between the recorded and the current hash as a reproducibility caveat (not a failure), so that a delivered board can always be tied to the code that made it.
