# 3. Benchmark Specification

Design the following converter:

```yaml
converter:
  topology: synchronous_buck
  input_voltage_nominal: 48V
  output_voltage: 24V
  output_power: 1000W

controller:
  preferred_device: TMS320F28335

pcb:
  architecture: integrated_power_and_control_board
  cad_tool: KiCad
```

Nominal output current is approximately:

```text
1000 W / 24 V ≈ 41.7 A
```

The intended power stage is a conventional synchronous buck using high-side and low-side switching devices.

# 22. AIPE PCB Engineering Rules

Begin building a reusable engineering-rule layer inside:

```text
docs/pcb-design-rules.md
```

and, where practical, a machine-readable rules representation.

Initial examples:

```text
PE-PCB-001
Minimize MOSFET gate loop.

PE-PCB-002
Place high-frequency DC-link capacitor close to the half bridge.

PE-PCB-003
Minimize high-frequency commutation-loop area.

PE-PCB-004
Use appropriate Kelvin routing for current measurement.

PE-PCB-005
Keep sensitive analog signals away from the switching node.

PE-PCB-006
Size high-current copper and vias for electrical and thermal requirements.

PE-PCB-007
Physically separate control electronics from noisy switching structures.

PE-PCB-008
Place IC decoupling capacitors adjacent to their relevant power pins.
```

Do not pretend every rule can already be checked numerically.

Mark rules as either:

```text
automated

semi-automated

human-review
```
